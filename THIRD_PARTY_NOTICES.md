# Third-party source in Mighty

Mighty modifies Hermes Desktop and includes public OpenHuman components. Original notices remain in place; third-party ownership is not transferred to Mighty.

## Hermes Desktop

Source: https://github.com/fathah/hermes-desktop . The original MIT license is preserved in LICENSE.

## OpenHuman

Source: https://github.com/tinyhumansai/openhuman , revision `1f58d88c5c2216e8019488b76c92cf1e3d42806d`.

The theme presets, theme token types, base tokens and Ghosty modules under `src/renderer/src/vendor/openhuman` come from that public source. Its GPL-3.0 license is included at `licenses/OpenHuman-GPL-3.0.txt`. Mighty adapters, layouts and workflows are separate modifications. Distributors of this combined edition must comply with GPL-3.0, including providing corresponding source and retaining notices. The original Hermes MIT notice does not cover the copied OpenHuman files.

## Artwork

The application artwork was supplied by the user for Mighty. The original composition is retained. No ownership of third-party imagery is claimed.

## Independent integrations

The local runtime may load Agency Agents' native router and Agent Reach. Their source repositories and original licenses accompany their separate installations. They do not replace the user's permissions or connected application APIs.
